# Outreach message

Write a short DM or email to a recruiter / hiring manager.

## Inputs

- profile identity + tutoring notes (tone)
- job title, company, optional team name
- chosen angle + one proof project
- locale

## Style

Follow `corpus/examples/outreach-message.md`.

Max ~120 words before the signature. One concrete proof. Soft CTA. No flattery paragraph.
