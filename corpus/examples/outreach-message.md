# Outreach message pattern

Keep it short. One proof. Soft CTA. No CV paraphrase wall.

```
Olá, equipe {COMPANY}!

Sou o {NAME} e me candidatei à vaga de {ROLE}.

{1–2 sentences: angle-aligned background + how you work}

Um exemplo concreto é {PROJECT}: {one concrete outcome/stack}.

Gostaria de contribuir com {TEAM_OR_PROBLEM}. Fico à disposição para conversar!

Abraço,
{NAME}
{PHONE}
{EMAIL}
{LINKEDIN}
```

## Anti-patterns

- Opening with “I hope this email finds you well”
- Listing every job
- Flattering the company with empty praise
- Sounding like a cover-letter essay
